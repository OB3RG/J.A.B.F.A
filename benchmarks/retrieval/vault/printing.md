# Printing notes

## PETG adhesion

Use a clean build plate and moderate first-layer speed. Record temperatures and fan settings for each filament batch.

## Maintenance

Inspect the nozzle, belts, and build surface on a regular schedule. Keep a short maintenance log with dates and observed symptoms. This material is deliberately unrelated to software retrieval.

## Filament storage

Seal filament with desiccant when it is not in use. Dry spools that show signs of moisture. These paragraphs provide realistic distractor text for the retrieval benchmark.
