# Cooking notes

## Bread

Weigh ingredients, observe fermentation rather than following only the clock, and record ambient temperature. Adjust hydration gradually when changing flour.

## Soup

Build flavor with aromatics and stock, then season near the end. Save successful ratios and substitutions. This note is another distractor and should not appear in evidence about software agents.

## Shopping

Check staples before shopping and keep a reusable list. Group ingredients by storage requirements and plan meals around perishable items first.
